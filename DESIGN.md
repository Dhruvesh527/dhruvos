# DhruvOS design system

## Scene
An employer or client opens a developer’s personal workspace on their own laptop. The graphite display, warm folders, and crisp blue selection convey the approved computer experience in everyday ambient light.

## Strategy
Approved graphite + blue + amber palette. Typography: Inter interface and JetBrains Mono for short machine messages. Original geometric blue wallpaper, sculpted amber folders, a restrained Windows-inspired shell. Solid app reading surfaces.

## Layout
Desktop icons at left; Projects open centrally after boot; taskbar below. Case study opens as a separate app above Projects. Phone uses a launcher and full-screen apps.

## Motion
Power → reveal → boot → screen zoom → desktop. Once per session, skippable, reduced-motion alternative. Fast app transitions without bounce.

## Components
Accessible AppWindow, FolderIcon, taskbar, breadcrumbs, project preview, DocumentViewer, contact actions, settings, simple view.
