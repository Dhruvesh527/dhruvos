# DhruvOS

## Register
brand

## Users
Employers and freelance clients, confirmed by the user. They need evidence of contribution, delivery, and a direct contact path.

## Product Purpose
A personal computer-inspired portfolio: power on, reveal laptop, boot, zoom into a working desktop, explore projects and achievements through app windows.

## Brand Personality
Tactile, composed, curious. Familiar desktop affordances with a personal identity.

## Anti-references
Avoid inaccessible tiny desktop replicas on phones, forced long loading, invented results, decorative clutter, and navigation that requires double-clicking alone.

## Design Principles
Show the work immediately. Make every window predictable. Keep resume and contact accessible. Delight through one precise cinematic entrance. Share the same content across desktop apps and direct pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard and touch opening, visible focus, labelled controls, reduced motion, skip intro, simple view, ordinary readable direct routes.

## Authorization
User approved the complete building plan on 2026-10-12. Local implementation and review first; publishing follows review.
